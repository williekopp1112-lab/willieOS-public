Rules Encoded

sideline reminders

QB Run Rule

The quarterback cannot rush, even when a legal blitzer crosses the line. Build answers with fast throws, handoffs, or behind-LOS laterals.

Blitz Rule

Any blitzer must start 7 yards off the line of scrimmage. The red dashed guide marks that depth on every diagram.

Behind-LOS Lateral

The QB can lateral behind the line of scrimmage to another player. That player can pass or run, which unlocks option and trick calls.

5 Players Only

Every call is built for exactly five players on offense and exactly five on defense. No 6v6 spacing or extra rusher assumptions.

12U Teaching Point

Use motion, bunches, and tags, but give each player one job. If a route is a decoy, say it clearly in the huddle.

Defensive Language

Defensive calls use shaded zones, leverage notes, and a clear rusher rule so kids understand depth before the snap.
