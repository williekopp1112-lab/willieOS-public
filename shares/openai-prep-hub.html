Recruiter Call Playbook

  • This is a meet & greet — be warm, curious, and concise
  • Lead with the 60-second narrative above, let them pull for details
  • If they ask you to explain a technical concept simply: use MCP as your example — "It's like a universal adapter that lets AI tools talk to each other, the way USB-C standardized charging for all your devices"
  • Every answer connects back to "I've built this"
  • Ask YOUR questions early — shows you're evaluating them too
  • Goal: get to the next round where you can dig deeper on technical/GTM alignment

Don't Say

  • "Gary costs $15/week" — makes it sound like a hobby
  • Mentioning Anthropic applications — don't volunteer this
  • Unverified market stats — stick to what you can defend
  • "Side project" framing — say "self-driven initiative"
  • Over-explaining the tech stack — focus on what it does, not how
  • Trashing competitors — respect the landscape
