Field FAQ

Answers before the first customer call.

Short, partner-safe language for early conversations. Deep architecture questions should go to the Megaport SA.

Is Latitude replacing Megaport NaaS?

No. Treat Latitude as an attach motion. Partners still lead with network outcomes, then add dedicated compute and storage when the workload needs it.

When should I bring in a Megaport SA?

Bring in an SA after one clear signal or whenever the customer asks about GPUs, bare metal sizing, storage performance, migration timing, or compliance architecture.

Can a Tier 1 partner still earn on the deal?

Yes. Tier 1 is designed for referral participation. The partner spots the deal, makes the intro, registers it, and earns through the referral model.

What is the easiest first workload?

Start with a bounded workload: GPU proof of concept, private cloud extension, migration landing zone, regulated app tier, or a known Equinix Metal migration candidate.

What if the customer asks for exact pricing?

Use the pricing calculator for a budget band, then confirm with Megaport SA. Do not treat the first estimate as a formal quote.
