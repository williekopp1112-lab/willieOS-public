◆ Red Flags to Watch

⚠
High CRO Turnover
If the CRO is new or this is the 2nd+ CRO, the org may be unstable. Ask how long they've been in seat.
⚠
Unrealistic Quotas
PE-backed = aggressive targets. Push for specifics on quota vs. historical attainment. 120%+ ramp expectations are a red flag.
⚠
No Equity Component
At a PE-backed company, equity is the upside. If there's no equity/options in the comp, the economics don't match the risk.
⚠
Unclear Exit Timeline
PE firms typically exit in 3–5 years. If they can't articulate the timeline, the thesis may be shaky or you could get caught in a transition.
⚠
Too Many Direct Reports
More than 8–10 direct reports means you're managing, not leading. Ask about the org structure and whether there are managers in between.
⚠
Heavy Travel, No Adjustment
"Western region" can mean constant flights. If travel is 60%+ with no travel comp or remote flexibility, that's a lifestyle concern.
