60-Second Pitch (Recruiter-Adapted)

17 years in enterprise tech sales. Cisco, then Nokia. At Cisco I ran the Sports & Entertainment CTO practice. SoFi Stadium, NFL, NBA, NASCAR. Multi-year, multi-million dollar deals with complex stakeholder maps. At Nokia I'm selling private 5G and edge compute to industrial enterprises.

The reason I'm excited about OpenAI specifically: I built my own AI operating system. Production software with 242 tools, multi-agent dispatch, autonomous workflows. It manages my email, calendar, tasks, and deliverables. I built it because I wanted to understand what it actually feels like to operationalize AI, not just sell it.

That means when I sit with a CTO or a VP of Engineering at a Fortune 500, I'm not reading a feature sheet. I can talk about token economics, orchestration patterns, failure modes, and governance from firsthand experience. I want to bring that to OpenAI's enterprise customers.

Key Talking Points

  • Career credibility: 17 years enterprise tech (Cisco 2006-2023, Nokia 2023-present). CTO-level relationships, multi-million dollar deal cycles
  • Why OpenAI, why now: The Agents SDK update signals OpenAI is serious about the enterprise agentic layer. I've been building on this stack.
  • Builder differentiator: WillieOS/Gary has 242 registered MCP tools, 4 specialist agents, autonomous workflows, and a full governance layer
  • Enterprise AI insight: AI sales is fundamentally different. Probabilistic product, buyers have no mental model. Sell evaluation frameworks and architecture durability, not features.
  • Cross-functional selling: Different pitch per persona. COO (cycle time), CFO (cost of delay), GC (auditability), CIO/CISO (data posture), CRO (rep productivity)
  • Proof of work: Built full account plans for John Deere and Kaiser Permanente unprompted
