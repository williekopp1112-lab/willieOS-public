Competitive Insight from a Builder

  • Multi-model is the default: DN companies don't go exclusive. They route based on cost, quality, and latency. Willie does this in production. He can have an honest conversation about where OpenAI fits in a multi-provider stack.
  • Primary model positioning: The play isn't "only use OpenAI." It's "make OpenAI the primary model for your highest-value workloads." Willie understands this because he routes expensive tasks to Claude Opus and cheap tasks to Gemini Flash.
  • Open-source pressure: DN companies evaluate fine-tuned open models for cost-sensitive tasks. Understanding where hosted APIs win (reliability, compliance, function calling quality) vs where OSS is good enough is critical.
