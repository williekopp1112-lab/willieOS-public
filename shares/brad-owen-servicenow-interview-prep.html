ServiceNow OT Market Context

The Market Opportunity

The OT security and asset management market is projected to grow from $23.47 billion in 2025 to $50.29 billion by 2030 at a 16.5% CAGR.3 Driving factors: increasing cyber threats to industrial systems, IT/OT convergence mandates, regulatory requirements (TSA, NERC CIP), and digital transformation in manufacturing and energy sectors.

ServiceNow's Unique Angle

ServiceNow positions OT Management as asset visibility + workflow orchestration on the Now Platform, not standalone security. Key differentiators:

  • • CMDB as System of Record: OT assets visualized alongside IT assets following ISA-95 Purdue Model4
  • • Service Graph Connectors: Integrates with Claroty, Dragos, Nozomi for asset discovery5
  • • Platform Leverage: Workflows for vulnerability remediation, change management, incident response built on existing ITOM/SecOps investments
  • • Armis Acquisition ($7.75B): Adds cyber exposure management across IT, OT, IoT, and medical devices6

Competitive Landscape

Claroty

Pure-play OT security platform. Strong in asset discovery and vulnerability management. Positioning: "We do OT security; ServiceNow does workflow."

Dragos

ICS threat detection and incident response. Strong in utilities and energy. Positioning: "We're purpose-built for OT; ServiceNow is IT-centric."

Nozomi Networks

Real-time OT/IoT visibility and anomaly detection. Strong in manufacturing. Positioning: "We're agentless and passive; ServiceNow requires integration."

Armis (being acquired)

Cyber exposure across IT/OT/IoT/medical. ServiceNow acquiring for $7.75B (H2 2026 close). Impact: Deepens OT asset discovery and risk scoring.

Tenable OT

Vulnerability management extended to OT. Positioning: "We're vulnerability-first; ServiceNow is workflow-first."

Microsoft Defender for IoT

Bundled with Azure. Positioning: "We're cloud-native; ServiceNow is hybrid and on-prem friendly."

Why OT is Strategic for ServiceNow

Security & Risk business crossed $1 billion ACV in Q3 2025.7 OT is a key growth driver alongside vulnerability response and threat intelligence. Bill McDermott's thesis: enterprises want one system of action for IT and OT, not point tools. Red Flags / Watch-Outs

❌ Don't Confuse OT with IoT

OT (Operational Technology) = industrial control systems (SCADA, PLCs, DCS) in factories, plants, utilities. IoT (Internet of Things) = connected consumer/enterprise devices. They overlap but are not the same. Brad will notice if you conflate them.

❌ Don't Position ServiceNow as Pure OT Security

ServiceNow is asset visibility + workflow orchestration, not standalone security. Claroty and Dragos are security tools. ServiceNow integrates with them via Service Graph Connectors. The value prop is workflow, not threat detection.

❌ Don't Bad-Mouth Competitors

Claroty, Dragos, and Nozomi are partners (via Service Graph Connectors) as much as competitors. Be respectful. The positioning is: "They're great at OT security. ServiceNow adds workflow and platform integration on top."

❌ Don't Overpromise on Day-One Productivity

You have OT domain credibility, but you'll need to ramp on ServiceNow's product, messaging, and internal processes. Be confident but realistic: "I can leverage my OT relationships to build pipeline quickly, but I'll need to learn your competitive positioning and product roadmap."

❌ Don't Assume You Know the Role

Brad is interviewing you, but the specific role isn't confirmed (AE? SE? Sales leader?). Ask early in the conversation: "Before we dive in, can you clarify the role you're hiring for? I want to make sure I'm addressing the right responsibilities."

❌ Don't Skip the 'Why ServiceNow?' Question

This is a layup. Have a crisp answer ready: platform story + Armis acquisition + Security & Risk crossing $1B ACV + Now Assist AI integration. Don't wing it.
