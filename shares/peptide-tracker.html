Quick Reference
  • Unusual water retention or bloating
  • Joint pain or stiffness (beyond normal)
  • Fasting glucose climbing above baseline
  • Persistent fatigue or lethargy
  • Numbness or tingling in hands/feet
  • Headaches that don't resolve
  • Injection site reactions (redness, lumps)

If any of these persist for 3+ days, pause protocol and consult your provider.

Week 1-2: Ramp-Up
  • Possible mild fatigue as body adjusts
  • Improved sleep quality (from CJC/Ipa)
  • Slight water retention — normal and temporary
Week 2-4: Early Benefits
  • Better sleep depth and recovery
  • Reduced inflammation at injury sites (BPC-157)
  • Skin/hair quality improvements (GHK-Cu)
Week 4-8: Peak Adaptation
  • Noticeable body composition changes
  • Accelerated healing of nagging injuries
  • Enhanced workout recovery
  • Improved energy and mental clarity
Week 8-12: Full Benefits
  • Significant tissue repair and healing
  • Stabilized body composition gains
  • Robust immune function improvement
  • Time to evaluate continuation vs cycling off
GLOW Blend (Morning)
  • Inject fasted — first thing in the morning
  • Wait 20-30 minutes before eating
  • Water and black coffee are fine beforehand
CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin (Evening)
  • Must be 2+ hours fasted before injection
  • No food for 30 minutes after injection
  • Best administered right before bed
  • Avoid high-sugar meals in the hours before
  • Abdomen: 2 inches from navel, alternate left/right. Most common site, fastest absorption.
  • Inner Thigh: Upper inner thigh, alternate legs. Good for those with less abdominal tissue.
  • Near Injury: For GLOW blend — inject subcutaneously near the injury site for localized healing. Keep 1-2 inches away from the actual wound/inflammation.

Rotate sites each day. Never inject the same exact spot two days in a row. The app tracks your last 3 sites and suggests the next rotation.
