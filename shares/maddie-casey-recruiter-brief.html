Guardrails

Do Not

✕

Don't monologue

Keep answers under 90 seconds. Pause. Let her drive.

✕

Don't mention other applications

Especially not Anthropic. OpenAI recruiters notice.

✕

Don't say "side project"

Say "self-driven initiative" or "a system I built." Side project trivializes it.

✕

Don't lead with comp questions

If she asks, answer honestly. But never bring it up first.

✕

Don't trash Nokia or current employer

Frame the move as "toward something" not "away from something."

✕

Don't over-explain WillieOS architecture

She's not technical. Say what it does, not how it works. "Multi-agent AI system" is enough.
