Your Story (The 60-Second Narrative)

Deliver this concisely, then let her pull for details.

The narrative (Digital Native Core version)

"I've spent 15+ years in enterprise tech sales at Cisco and Nokia, selling complex platforms to Fortune 500 buyers. But what makes me different for this role isn't the enterprise background. It's what I've been building. I built Gary, a production AI operating system. It runs on multi-model architecture: routing tasks across Claude, GPT, and Gemini based on cost and capability. It has 110+ MCP tools, autonomous agent dispatch, consumption-based cost controls, and guardrail systems that cap API spend. It is the exact kind of system your Digital Native Core customers are building right now. I understand the digital native buyer because I am one. I know what token economics look like in production. I know why model routing matters. And I bring the enterprise sales discipline to help these companies scale their OpenAI usage from experimentation to production commitment."

Guardrails

Do Not

✕
Don't monologue
Keep answers under 90 seconds. Pause. Let her drive.
✕
Don't mention other applications
Especially not Anthropic. OpenAI recruiters notice.
✕
Don't say "side project"
Say "self-driven initiative" or "a system I built." Side project trivializes it.
✕
Don't lead with comp questions
If she asks, answer honestly. But never bring it up first.
✕
Don't trash Nokia or current employer
Frame the move as "toward something" not "away from something."
✕
Don't over-explain WillieOS architecture
She's not technical. Say what it does, not how. "Multi-agent AI system" is enough.
