The Storage Stack — Keep It Simple

Two Options. Full Stop.

One of the fastest ways to lose a deal is to overwhelm sellers with options. Latitude's storage story is clean: VAST for performance workloads, Wasabi for archival. That's the answer to every storage question until the customer is in a 301-level design session.

VAST Data
Performance & Flash
  • AI / ML training and inference workloads
  • High-throughput databases
  • Media rendering and post-production
  • Any workload where latency is measured in microseconds
  • Ask: "Is your team bottlenecked by storage IOPS?"
Wasabi
Archival & Cold Storage
  • Backup and disaster recovery
  • Compliance archive (HIPAA, SEC, FINRA)
  • Long-term media and log retention
  • S3-compatible — simple migration from AWS S3
  • Ask: "How much are you spending on AWS S3 or Glacier?"
