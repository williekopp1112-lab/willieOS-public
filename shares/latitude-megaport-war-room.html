04 - Channel enablement

The channel cannot sell what it cannot explain in one page.

If 70% of Megaport motion runs through partners, Latitude enablement has to be partner-native from the start. Not a seller deck repurposed as a PDF. A real partner operating kit.

Certification Program

Latitude Certified Partner with Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers. Online modules for positioning, hands-on labs for deployment, and a practical exam where the partner builds a bare metal + Megaport architecture.

Co-Selling Motions

Joint account planning with dedicated Latitude overlay specialists for channel. The overlay does first 3 deals with the partner, then shifts into coaching mode.

Deal Registration

First-to-register gets protected margin, starting at 15-20% and escalating with deal size, multi-year term, and storage/network attach.

Partner Portal Content

Battlecards, ROI calculator, reference architectures, customer-facing one-pagers, migration checklists, and "first meeting" discovery scripts.

06 - Comp architecture

Incentives have to evolve before the field treats Latitude as core.

Spiffs can start motion, but quota creates muscle memory. The bridge needs to be clear enough for leadership, sellers, SAs, and channel partners to understand what changes and when.

Phase 1 - Now

Spiff-only launch motion

Keep $500-$2K per closed deal as the near-term spark while enablement and qualification catch up.

$500-$2K
Phase 2 - Q3

Quota-eligible overlay

Make Latitude 10-15% of total quota for applicable roles, with overlay support for account teams.

10-15%
Phase 3 - Q4+

Full quota inclusion

Full quota credit with accelerators. Deals above $100K receive a 2x multiplier when they include network and storage attach.

2x

Channel margin recommendations

Protect margin for registered deals, escalate for larger commitments, and pay extra when partners include Megaport fabric and storage attach instead of compute-only resale.

Quarterly channel spiffs

Run "first Latitude deal," "largest Latitude deal," and "most Latitude deals" incentives every quarter. Keep the mechanic simple enough that partners remember it.
