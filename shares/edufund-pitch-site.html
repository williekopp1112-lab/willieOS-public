The Problem

Millions Left on the Table Every Year

California school districts run on thin budgets, yet most lack the tools to capture every dollar they've already earned.

Unclaimed ADA Revenue

When students are absent, districts can recover funding through ADA recovery programs. But manual tracking with spreadsheets means most eligible sessions are never scheduled, and $300K-$600K+ per year goes unclaimed.

Mental Health Billing Gaps

CYBHI (Children and Youth Behavioral Health Initiative) allows Medi-Cal reimbursement for school-based mental health services. But complicated billing codes and compliance requirements leave most districts unable to capture this revenue.

Google Sheets Infrastructure

Most districts track absences, recovery sessions, and compliance in disconnected spreadsheets. No automation, no real-time visibility, no audit trail. Staff spend hours on data entry instead of serving students.

Compliance Risk

ADA recovery and Medi-Cal billing require strict documentation. Manual processes introduce errors, missed deadlines, and audit exposure. One compliance failure can cost more than the revenue recovered.

How It Works

From Absence to Revenue in 6 Steps

Mental Health Claims Module

Turn Student Support Into Sustainable Revenue

$200K–$1M+ / year

California's CYBHI initiative allows schools to bill Medi-Cal for mental health services delivered on campus. Most districts are leaving this revenue uncaptured because billing is complex. EduFund automates the entire claims workflow.

  • Automated session documentation with billing code mapping
  • Real-time eligibility verification against Medi-Cal enrollment
  • Batch claim submission with error checking and rejection management
  • Provider credential tracking and NPI validation
  • FERPA and HIPAA compliant data handling throughout
  • Revenue dashboard with per-provider and per-school breakdowns

CYBHI Billing Workflow

Student Receives Service

Counselor, therapist, or social worker provides an eligible mental health service on campus

Auto-Document & Code

EduFund maps the session to the correct CPT/HCPCS billing code and generates compliant documentation

Verify Eligibility

Real-time check against Medi-Cal enrollment. Ineligible students are flagged before submission.

Submit & Track Claims

Batch claims submitted to Medi-Cal with full audit trail. Rejections auto-routed for correction and resubmission.

Revenue Captured

Reimbursements flow back to the district. Dashboard tracks revenue per provider, per school, per service type.
