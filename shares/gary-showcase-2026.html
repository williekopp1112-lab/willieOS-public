Gary

An AI Chief of Staff that manages email, calendar, tasks, research, design, and deployment—autonomously.

What Gary Does

Not a chatbot. An operating system layer.

Gary sits between the user and every digital surface they touch. Email arrives and Gary triages it before you wake up. Calendar events trigger pre-meeting research briefs. Tasks get prioritized by deadline, leverage, and who they block.

When you need something built—a presentation, a research report, a code feature, a design mockup—Gary dispatches the right specialist agent, monitors the work, validates the output against acceptance criteria, and delivers the result. You describe what you need. Gary handles the how.

What Makes Gary Different

Four architectural principles

Local-First
All data stays on your machine in SQLite. No cloud backend, no data exfiltration. AI APIs are the only external calls, and those are configurable.
Multi-Model
Uses Claude, GPT, and Gemini where each excels. Claude for reasoning. GPT for image generation. Gemini Flash for high-volume pattern matching. Per-service routing via settings.
Autonomous
Runs workflows without prompting. Email triage happens before you check your inbox. Meeting briefs generate before the meeting starts. Cost monitoring catches anomalies overnight.
Self-Improving
Tracks corrections, identifies recurring patterns, promotes validated learnings to permanent rules. Weekly reflection synthesizes insights and adjusts behavior.
