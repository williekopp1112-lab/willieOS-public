Kaiser Permanente is the largest integrated health system in the United States: 12.6M+ members, 40 hospitals, 600+ medical offices, $127.7B in operating revenue (2025). Governance & Risk Plan

Healthcare AI procurement involves layers of compliance, clinical validation, and organizational change management that do not exist in other verticals. This section maps the governance landscape an Account Director must navigate.

Domain Requirements & Considerations Status
HIPAA / BAA Business Associate Agreement required before any PHI processing. OpenAI offers HIPAA-eligible API access with BAA. Must cover: data transmission encryption, access controls, audit logging, breach notification procedures, data retention/deletion policies. Separate BAA needed for each deployment type (API, ChatGPT Enterprise, Codex). In Progress
Data Boundaries Zero training on patient data (contractual guarantee). Azure private deployment option for sensitive workloads. Data residency within US borders. No cross-customer data exposure. PHI must be de-identified or encrypted at rest and in transit. Separate data handling for insurer vs. provider operations. Available
Security SOC 2 Type II compliance, penetration testing reports, HITRUST certification pathway. SSO/SAML integration with Kaiser's identity provider. Role-based access controls aligned with clinical privilege levels. Network isolation for clinical AI workloads. Regular third-party security audits. Available
Clinical Validation Kaiser has an established AI quality assurance program through their Division of Research. Any clinical AI deployment requires: prospective clinical validation study, accuracy benchmarking against human performance, bias testing across demographic groups, ongoing monitoring framework, physician attestation workflow. Kaiser's VP of AI oversees this process. Required
Change Management Physician adoption program: champion network, peer training, workflow integration support. 88% of physicians in Kaiser's Abridge pilot reported positive experience, setting favorable precedent. Union engagement required for any workforce-impacting deployments (contact center, administrative automation). Regional rollout strategy across 8 states with different regulatory environments. Planning
Measurement Framework: physician time saved per encounter, call deflection rate, average handle time reduction, prior auth turnaround time, HEDIS quality score impact, patient satisfaction (CAHPS) correlation, cost per transaction. Quarterly business reviews with executive sponsors. ROI validation at 6-month and 12-month checkpoints. Defined
Procurement Multi-year enterprise agreement structure. Volume pricing tiers based on API consumption and seat count. Phased deployment aligned with clinical validation milestones. Pilot-to-production pricing with committed minimums. Potential inclusion in Risant Health master agreement for cross-system leverage (Geisinger, Cone Health). Typical Kaiser enterprise procurement cycle: 6-12 months. TBD
