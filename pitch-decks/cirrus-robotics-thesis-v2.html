PART 2 — REBUILT THESIS

The pitch, rewritten so it survives scrutiny

Cirrus has spent two decades shipping precision analog at smartphone volumes for the world's most demanding customer. That capability — not any one product line — is what humanoid robot platforms need now, in three specific places where Cirrus has differentiated FY26 product, not legacy story.

1 · Robot perception — anchored on the industrial imaging AFE family

In April 2026, Cirrus launched the CS82L41 / CS82L44 / CS82L46 industrial imaging analog front-end family with redesigned SAR ADC architecture, integrated LED drive, and CMOS/LVDS outputs for high-precision scan systems. This is the strongest robotics anchor Cirrus has — already industrial-grade, already designed for machine-vision-class pipelines, and a clear roadmap from "flatbed scanner" to "robot perception subsystem" without inventing a new product line. Pair with Cirrus's in-design smart-power IC for 3D sensing (Face ID-class) and you have a credible perception-AFE story.

2 · Tactile feedback & fine actuation — anchored on automotive haptics

The CS40L51/52/53 family is already automotive-qualified closed-loop haptic drivers. Industrial / humanoid reliability targets sit between consumer and automotive — Cirrus's auto parts are the right beachhead, not the consumer Pixel-grade parts. Position around fingertip tactile sensing and force feedback for teleoperation, not joint actuation (which is a different motor problem).

3 · Audio I/O for human-robot interaction — the franchise

Humanoid robots need to hear well in noisy environments, do beamforming for directional voice, and produce natural speech. Cirrus's audio codec + amplifier stack is the most production-proven HRI audio chain on earth. This is the easy win — same IP, same supply chain, immediate plug-in for any humanoid that wants to skip rolling their own audio.

4 · Power & battery management — the under-told story

Cirrus has been quietly building advanced battery and power IP across recent earnings calls. Humanoid robots are battery-bound — runtime is the #1 commercial blocker (Optimus targets 8-hour shifts). Cirrus's smart-power work is more relevant than the smartphone-camera analogy.

PART 3 — COMPETITIVE REALITY CHECK

The robotics platform conversation is already happening — without Cirrus

This is the uncomfortable part the original document didn't address, and it's where leadership will push back hardest. In March 2026, NVIDIA announced robotics platform partnerships with Texas Instruments, NXP, Analog Devices, Infineon, and Synopsys at GTC. NXP's humanoid building blocks ship 1H 2026. TI's AM13Ex MCUs integrate motor control + edge AI on a single die. Cirrus is not in this club.

The reframe: Don't pitch Cirrus as the platform. Pitch Cirrus as the differentiated specialty silicon that gets designed in alongside those platforms — the way Cirrus codecs are designed in alongside Qualcomm SoCs in phones. The platform fight is over; the specialty-IP fight is wide open and starts in BOM lock-down windows over the next 12-24 months.
